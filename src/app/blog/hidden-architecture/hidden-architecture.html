The Cryptographic Triad: SPF, DKIM, DMARC

Setting up a domain is just the start. Managing its sender reputation requires the three‑step Cryptographic Triad: 

[Outbound Email] → [SPF Verification] → [DKIM Signature] → [DMARC Policy] → [Primary Inbox]
  • SPF – A DNS ledger that authorizes specific IPs to send mail for your domain.
  • DKIM – Adds an immutable cryptographic signature to each message.
  • DMARC – Enforces policy (none → quarantine → reject) and provides reporting.
