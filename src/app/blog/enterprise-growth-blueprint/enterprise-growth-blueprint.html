Pillar 5: Cryptographic IT Infrastructure — The Foundation Everything Depends On

The most sophisticated automation and marketing strategies fail silently if your foundational IT infrastructure is broken. The most common invisible failure is email deliverability: if your emails land in spam, you are losing revenue without knowing it.

The fix is cryptographic: SPF (authorises sending IPs), DKIM (proves message integrity), and DMARC (enforces policy). Without all three perfectly aligned, ISPs route your mail to spam regardless of content quality.

  • A B2B SaaS firm raised sender reputation from 65 to 98 after DNS alignment.
  • A fintech company achieved a permanent 30% inbox placement improvement.
  • A telecom enterprise safely sent 620,000 emails per day after a 21-day governed warmup.
