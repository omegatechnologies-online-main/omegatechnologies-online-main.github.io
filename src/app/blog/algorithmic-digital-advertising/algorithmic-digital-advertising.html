The era of manual media buying is over. Previously, human operators spent hours manually adjusting keyword bids by fractions of a cent. Today, rapid market penetration and real‑time demand generation require highly efficient, algorithmic digital advertising.

This shift is championed by machine learning frameworks like Google’s Performance Max (PMax). Officially replacing legacy Smart Shopping models, PMax consolidates all available network inventory into a singular, automated hub. Advertisers can now seamlessly distribute creative assets simultaneously across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail, and Maps.
