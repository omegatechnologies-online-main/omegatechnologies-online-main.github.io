# Omega Technologies — AI Automation, Programmatic SEO & Enterprise Growth Engineering

Omega Technologies builds scalable AI automation, programmatic SEO engines, and enterprise digital infrastructure. We engineer systems that scale companies, automate lead flows, guarantee email deliverability, and optimize ad spend.

## Core Capabilities & Services

- **AI Workflow Automation & Multi-Agent Systems**: Deploying custom agentic architectures using n8n, LangGraph, RAG, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) to automate complex operations and reduce costs.
- **Programmatic SEO (pSEO)**: Designing and developing programmatic search capture engines to drive millions of high-intent organic visitors to enterprise platforms.
- **Domain Trust & Cryptographic Email Deliverability**: Rescuing and building outbound sender reputation via cryptographic protocols (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), horizontal scaling, and AI warmups.
- **Algorithmic Digital Advertising**: Building automated ad creation, creative testing, and multi-network media buying pipelines targeting conversion.
- **RAG-Grounded Cultural Localization**: Combining translation with vector-based context alignment to localize enterprise assets for global markets.
